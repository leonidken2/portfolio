Marketing strategy template based on Kotler

The template I build strategies with: from the brief and research to 7P, a plan and KPIs.

My working template in Google Sheets

Open the template (in Russian)

Eight sheets, eight steps

Brief → research → segmentation → competitors → positioning → 7P, plan and KPIs

  1. 01
    Brief and growth hypotheses

    Goal, resources, constraints, growth model

  2. 02
    Observation

    Desk research, SWOT, micro and macro environment

  3. 03
    Experiment

    Tests, timing, budget, success criteria

  4. 04
    Survey and customer interviews

    Questionnaires, interview guide, analysis

  5. 05
    Segmentation

    Behavior, motives, the core audience

  6. 06
    Competitors and needs

    A map of the market and the alternatives

  7. 07
    Positioning

    Insights → platform, message, reasons to believe

  8. 08
    7P, plan and KPIs

    Channels, content, budget, a monthly plan

Two rules of the template

No channels or content until the goal and the growth logic are fixed.

Positioning comes only after research insights, not from gut feeling.
