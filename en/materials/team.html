How I grade a test task

The same criteria for every candidate

  • Did the writer get the product, any factual errors
  • Structure and headline: the point lands in seconds
  • Readability and clarity scores (Glavred)
  • Filler and keyword stuffing
  • AI-generation check
  • Deadline and experience in healthcare

How I write a brief

The contractor shouldn't have to ask

  • Context: what the product is and why the task matters
  • The audience and the focus for each
  • The main task in one sentence
  • What to deliver: format, length, structure
  • Constraints, e.g. don't change the meaning of medical wording
  • Source materials and questions to clear

Two briefs from practice

  • For a copywriter

    Education materials on a medical test for three audiences: doctors, medical reps and corporate account managers. Deliver the split logic, the structure of three decks, the first 5 slides of each with slide copy and speaker notes, and questions for the medical team.

  • For a video editor

    Cut a 90-minute webinar shot on two cameras plus slides into a YouTube video: sync, camera switching, slides where they belong, pauses removed, captions. No heavy graphics, don't change the meaning of medical wording. Budget ₽3,000.

A 30-day work plan

Eight workstreams in one period

  1. 01Doctor training with a partner lab
  2. 02A Telegram channel for doctors
  3. 03A webinar with a guest expert
  4. 04YouTube and Instagram concept
  5. 05Pink October: content campaign
  6. 06Digital health calendar and website
  7. 07Merch and print
  8. 08Conference: handouts, video, booth

Every task has an owner and a deadline. At the end of the period, a report per workstream: status, what got done, the next step.
