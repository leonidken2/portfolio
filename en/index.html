OncoDiag: taking a cancer-diagnostics test to market

An innovative cancer-diagnostics product based on the CA-62 biomarker. There's no single buyer: the decision runs through doctors, laboratories and partners. So the work started with research, not with ads, and every next step gets tested in the field.

  • Wrote the research survey (260+ respondents) and ran 20 in-depth interviews myself
  • Turned the research into a problem funnel and narrowed 49 ideas to 10 priority GTM hypotheses on four criteria: product stage, feasibility, measurability, scalability
  • Finalized CA-62 positioning and briefed the 44-slide physician education program and the physician channel
  • Briefed copywriters and designers and saw the work through: Citilab front-desk scripts, the partnership landing page, the result form, cards, algorithms and clinical cases
  • From my briefs the team produced a 6-format partner education toolkit and 3 product presentations
  • Proposed and launched a Salebot chatbot: designed the flow and built it with a developer
  • At the PreventMed conference: a 14-slide deck, 200+ materials distributed, 3 clinic adoption scenarios. Doctors' questions went back into training
  • Now running a Pink October activation with Citilab: a digital health calendar, a QR funnel, lobby videos and a pool of 1,000 promo codes
  • Coordinate copywriters, designers and contractors

Minimum Innovative Dentistry: patients from three channels

A private dental clinic. The goal wasn't to “run ads” but to bring in patients and know how much revenue each channel produced. So ads, service pages, WhatsApp, appointments and the CRM worked as one chain.

  • 1,000+ new patients over 2 years across all channels: ads, Instagram, reviews and referrals
  • Built and managed 3 acquisition funnels across Google Ads, Instagram and organic search
  • Connected acquisition with landing pages, CRM, WhatsApp, appointments, patients and revenue
  • Built 13 service-specific landing pages around dental search demand and used them for both SEO and Google Ads: 17.6K sessions over the period
  • Ran dedicated Google Ads search campaigns per service: about 1.8K clicks straight to the service pages
  • Analysed site leads: 773 over the period, 294 named a source, and 32% of those came from Google or Yandex search
  • Managed Instagram paid campaigns: 637K+ impressions and 6.2K+ link clicks on about $1.1K spend
  • Developed marketing strategy for 5 audience segments
  • Built a content system with 6 content pillars and 36 Instagram posts
  • Produced photo and video content: doctors, cases, Reels and Stories
  • Grew Instagram to 2,000+ targeted followers

How I work

Four steps that repeat across every case

  1. 1

    Research

    Surveys and in-depth interviews before any budget is spent. I look for who decides and what stops them.

    OncoDiag: 260+ respondents, 20 interviews

  2. 2

    Position

    I define the value for each audience and turn it into hypotheses that can be tested.

    OncoDiag: 10 hypotheses out of 49 ideas

  3. 3

    Build channels

    Ads, SEO, content, partnerships and influencers. Each channel gets its own funnel and its own page.

    Dental: 13 service pages, 17.6K sessions

  4. 4

    Count revenue

    I link each inquiry to an appointment, a patient and a payment, so budget goes where it pays back.

    Google Ads: 7.9x ROAS
