OncoDiag: taking a cancer-diagnostics test to market

An innovative cancer-diagnostics product based on the CA-62 biomarker. There's no single buyer: the decision runs through doctors, laboratories and partners. So the work started with research, not with ads.

  • Ran customer research with 260+ respondents and 20 in-depth interviews
  • Developed product positioning and GTM strategy; created 10 GTM hypotheses
  • Built B2B marketing for doctors, laboratories and partners
  • Created a 6-format partner education toolkit and 3 product presentations
  • Supported educational activities for 100+ healthcare professionals
  • Coordinated copywriters, designers and project contributors

Minimum Innovative Dentistry: patients from three channels

A private dental clinic in Batumi. The goal wasn't to “run ads” but to bring in patients and know how much revenue each channel produced. So ads, service pages, WhatsApp, appointments and the CRM worked as one chain.

  • Built and managed 3 acquisition funnels across Google Ads, Instagram and organic search
  • Connected acquisition with landing pages, CRM, WhatsApp, appointments, patients and revenue
  • Managed Instagram paid campaigns: 637K+ impressions and 6.2K+ link clicks on about $1.1K spend
  • Built an SEO conversion funnel with 13 service-specific pages
  • Developed marketing strategy for 5 audience segments
  • Built a content system with 6 content pillars and 36 Instagram posts
  • Produced photo and video content: doctors, cases, Reels and Stories
  • Grew Instagram to 2,000+ targeted followers

Media and community project: from zero to 1,800 followers

My own Instagram project with a team of 10. The audience came from influencers, referral mechanics, brand partnerships and participants' video stories.

  • Ran takeovers: a guest blogger hosted the account for a day, ending with a joint live stream
  • Ran a charity campaign with 5 partner brands: 100+ comments, RUB 30,000 donated to a foundation
  • Produced 5 storytelling videos; the project was featured on TV
  • Led a cross-functional team of 10: weekly planning, tasks in a task manager

How I work

Four steps that repeat across every case

  1. 1

    Research

    Surveys and in-depth interviews before any budget is spent. I look for who decides and what stops them.

    OncoDiag: 260+ respondents, 20 interviews

  2. 2

    Position

    I define the value for each audience and turn it into hypotheses that can be tested.

    OncoDiag: 10 GTM hypotheses

  3. 3

    Build channels

    Ads, SEO, content, partnerships and influencers. Each channel gets its own funnel and its own page.

    Dental: 3 funnels, 13 SEO pages

  4. 4

    Count revenue

    I link each inquiry to an appointment, a patient and a payment, so budget goes where it pays back.

    Google Ads: 7.9x ROAS
